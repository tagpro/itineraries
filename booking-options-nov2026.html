Read this first

Four things the live timetables changed. The rest of this sheet already accounts for them.

The Mumbai afternoon flight doesn't exist

The plan says “Bangalore morning, fly Bangalore → Mumbai afternoon” on Wed 4 Nov. Every non-stop I found into Mumbai's main airport (BOM) is either morning (up to 10:20) or evening (from 19:30) — nothing lands in the afternoon. The one afternoon non-stop goes to Navi Mumbai (NMI), a different airport roughly 90 minutes from Colaba. You have to pick which half of the day to give up. Four ways to play it in Leg 3.

Chandigarh → Bangalore has more non-stops than you thought

The plan lists two IndiGo non-stops and says most other options involve a layover. There are actually five non-stops, including an 08:00 departure and two Air India Express services. The two flight times in the plan are also both wrong: 6E 429 arrives 19:40 (not 19:00) and 6E 593 runs 18:50–22:05 (not 19:50–22:20). The 08:00 flight is the one you want — it gets your parents to Bangalore before you.

The Nov 15 connection works — on exactly one flight

Seven non-stops run Chandigarh → Delhi that day. Three land after your 21:40 international departure, and one lands with under three hours to spare. AI 2454, 15:45–16:55, is the flight the plan was hoping for: a 4h45m buffer, and Air India domestic uses Terminal 3 — the same terminal your international flight leaves from, so no inter-terminal transfer. Book this one early.

The hotel budget is low by roughly 2–3×

Live rates run well above the estimates in the plan. Mumbai is the worst offender: Marine Plaza was pencilled at ₹8,000–9,500 a night and is quoting ₹23,871; the President at ₹8,700 is quoting ₹25,644. Delhi's Aloft was pencilled at ₹8,500 and is quoting ₹17,700. Good news is that the searches turned up better-value properties at every tier — they're marked below.

Book in this order

Two of these are time-sensitive; the rest can wait.

  1. AI 2454 on 15 Nov, four seats. The only Chandigarh–Delhi flight that combines a comfortable buffer with a Terminal 3 arrival. Three of the seven non-stops that day are unusable, so this is the scarcest seat on the trip.
  2. Decide the Mumbai arrival question and book Leg 3 to match. Morning departure buys you the Marine Drive sunset; evening departure buys you a second Bangalore breakfast. You can't have both.
  3. Leg 4, Mumbai → Chandigarh. The priciest leg, and the one where booking early pays best across four tickets.
  4. Legs 1 and 2 — both have ten and five non-stop options respectively with little price spread, so there's slack here. Just confirm the two arrivals still land within an hour of each other after you book.
  5. Mumbai hotel next, then Bangalore, then Delhi. Mumbai is the scarce, expensive one; the Delhi airport night can be booked a week out without penalty.
  6. Re-verify Leg 5's arrival terminal when you book, since it's a separate ticket from your international flight and a terminal change would eat the buffer.

Three things that clash with the plan

Opening hours and ferry schedules, checked against the days you're actually there.

Elephanta can't be an afternoon add-on

The plan lists Elephanta Caves as “optional if time allows” after a morning in Colaba and stops at Haji Ali and Bandra. It doesn't work that way: the last ferry out of the Gateway leaves at 14:00, the crossing is about an hour each way, and return ferries stop at 17:00. Elephanta is a morning commitment that eats most of the day — it replaces the Haji Ali and Bandra loop rather than fitting around it. Your Mumbai day is Thursday 5 Nov, so the Monday closure doesn't bite.

MTR is closed on Mondays

You land in Bangalore on Monday 2 Nov, so MTR is out for that evening — it only opens for you on Tuesday 3 Nov, which is where the plan already has it. Worth knowing if the days shuffle. It also shuts at 21:00 and closes between 11:00 and 12:30, so it's a late-breakfast or early-dinner place, not a late one.

The 08:30 flight rules out a second Taaza Thindi

Taaza Thindi opens at 07:00. With the recommended 08:30 Bangalore → Mumbai departure on 4 Nov you'd need to leave for the airport around 06:30, so that last breakfast is gone. The fix is easy though: Taaza Thindi also runs an evening session, 16:30–21:30. You land on 2 Nov at 13:25, so you can go that same evening and stop treating it as a breakfast-only problem.

Bangalore

Mon 2 Nov, from 13:25 · Tue 3 Nov, full day · Wed 4 Nov, early start

Two evenings and one full day. Lalbagh and Bangalore Palace are the two that need daylight; everything else is flexible.

Worth the time

Lalbagh Botanical Garden

06:00 – 19:00 daily

240 acres, a Victorian glass house, and a 3,000-million-year-old rock outcrop. Go early — it's a working park for the city before it's a sight. Nominal entry.

Bangalore Palace

10:00 – 17:30 daily

Tudor revival, built by the Wodeyars. Entry is steep by Indian standards and there's a separate camera fee. The audio guide is genuinely good.

Cubbon Park

Open access · car-free Sundays

Free, central, and the easiest thing to do on your first evening when nobody wants a schedule. The Seshadri Iyer library building is the photo.

ISKCON Temple, Rajajinagar

07:15 – 20:20, midday break

Hilltop complex, very large, very busy at weekends. Free entry. Modest dress; leave shoes and phones at the counters.

Commercial Street & MG Road

~11:00 – 21:00, most shops

Textiles, silver and street shopping on Commercial Street; MG Road is the more polished version. Both are close to the Indiranagar hotels.

Vidhana Soudha

Exterior only · floodlit weekends

The granite seat of the state legislature. No public entry, but it's lit up on Sunday evenings and worth a slow drive past.

Where to eat

Taaza Thindi · Jayanagar 4th Block

07:00 – 12:30 · 16:30 – 21:30

The one to prioritise. Masala dosa, idli-vada, chow chow bath with kesari bath, filter coffee — around ₹80 for two dishes each. Counter service, standing room, cash-friendly. Go early or in the evening; the midday gap is real.

MTR · Lalbagh Road

06:30 – 11:00 · 12:30 – 21:00 · closed Mon

Serving since 1924, and where rava idli was invented during wartime rice rationing. Come for the thali or the rava idli. Expect to queue and to be seated with strangers.

Shri Sagar (CTR) · Malleshwaram

07:00 – 12:30 · 16:00 – 21:00

The benne masala dosa — butter-drenched, crisp-edged, and the main argument against a second Taaza Thindi visit. Small, loud, worth it.

Vidyarthi Bhavan · Gandhi Bazaar

Breakfast & late afternoon

Basavanagudi institution since 1943, famous for its own take on the benne dosa. Queues out the door at weekends; a weekday morning is far easier.

Toit · Indiranagar

~12:00 – late

The microbrewery in the plan, and walking distance from the Indiranagar hotels. No reservations, and the wait after 19:00 is serious — go early or eat first.

Truffles · Koramangala

~11:00 – 23:00

Burgers and steaks, the other option in the plan. Long queues, and a 25-minute ride from Indiranagar — the weaker choice unless you're already south.

Mumbai

Wed 4 Nov, from ~11:00 · Thu 5 Nov, full day · Fri 6 Nov, departure

With the morning flight you gain a Mumbai afternoon and the Marine Drive sunset on day one. Then Thursday forks: Elephanta, or the city loop. Not both.

Worth the time

Gateway of India

Open access

Free, always crowded, and the anchor for everything else in Colaba. Also where the Elephanta ferries leave from, so you'll be here regardless.

Elephanta Caves

Ferries 09:00 – 14:00 out · closed Mon

7th-century rock-cut Shiva temples on an island an hour offshore. Round-trip ferry runs about ₹260, entry ₹40 for Indian nationals and ₹600 for foreign. Budget the whole morning and then some — last return is 17:00.

Marine Drive

Best 17:30 – 19:00

The sunset walk, and the reason the morning flight out of Bangalore earns its place. Chowpatty is at the northern end for chaat afterwards.

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus

Exterior anytime · floodlit at night

UNESCO-listed Victorian Gothic, and still a working commuter station. Best photographed from across the road at dusk.

Haji Ali Dargah

Causeway at low tide only

A tomb on an islet reached by a stone causeway that floods at high tide — check the tide before committing to the walk out.

Bandra

Late afternoon onward

Bandstand promenade, Mount Mary Church, and the street-art lanes off Chapel Road. Also where Elco Pani Puri is, so pair the two.

Where to eat

Elco Pani Puri Center · Bandra

~11:00 – 23:30

The pani puri stop from the plan. Sit-down section inside, street counter outside — the counter is the better version and the cheaper one.

Sardar Pav Bhaji · Tardeo

~12:00 – 01:30

The pav bhaji benchmark, and unapologetic about the amount of butter involved. Expect a wait and a token system at peak.

Aaram Vada Pav · near CST

~07:00 – 22:00

The classic vada pav, opposite CST, and about as quick a stop as food gets. Pairs naturally with photographing the terminus.

Bademiya · Colaba

Evening until very late

Seekh kebabs and rolls cooked on the street behind the Taj. A Colaba late-night institution — useful on the night you arrive tired.

Britannia & Co · Ballard Estate

Lunch only · limited days

Parsi, ancient, and famous for berry pulao. Lunch-only and closed some days, so ring ahead rather than turning up hopefully.

Girgaon Chowpatty

Late afternoon into the night

Bhelpuri, sevpuri, and kulfi falooda to finish, right at the end of the Marine Drive walk. This is the plan's chaat dinner.

Chandigarh

Fri 6 – Sun 15 Nov · nine days, no fixed plan

The unstructured stretch. Three or four days of city sights, and enough time left over for a real trip into the hills if you want one.

In the city

Rock Garden of Nek Chand

09:00 – 19:00 · shorter in winter

Forty acres built secretly from industrial and household waste over eighteen years. Genuinely strange and the single best thing in Chandigarh. Small entry fee.

Sukhna Lake

Open access · best at sunrise

Man-made lake at the foot of the Shivaliks, with a long promenade. Free, and the default morning walk for the whole city.

Capitol Complex

Free guided tours · photo ID required

Le Corbusier's UNESCO-listed government buildings and the Open Hand monument. Access is by escorted tour only and needs ID — arrange it a day ahead.

Zakir Hussain Rose Garden

Daylight hours

Asia's largest rose garden, 1,600 varieties across 30 acres. November is decent but not peak — the big bloom is February.

Sector 17 Plaza

Shops ~11:00 – 21:00

The pedestrian market at the centre of Le Corbusier's grid, with the fountain and plenty of places to sit. Sindhi Sweets is here.

Government Museum & Art Gallery

10:00 – 16:30 · closed Mon

Gandharan sculpture and Pahari miniatures, in another Corbusier building. Small, quiet, and a good rainy-afternoon option.

Day trips, if the nine days start to drag

Pinjore Gardens

~30 min by road

Seventeenth-century Mughal terraced garden at Kalka. Half a day, easy with parents, and lit up in the evenings.

Kasauli

~1.5 hrs by road

Small colonial hill station in the Shivaliks. Cool November air, pine walks, and doable as a long day trip without an overnight.

Amritsar

~4 hrs by road or a short flight

The Golden Temple and the Wagah border ceremony. Really an overnight rather than a day trip — but you have the days spare for it.

Shimla

~3.5 hrs by road

The Kalka–Shimla toy train is the reason to go, and November is clear and cold. Needs two days to be worth the drive.

Where to eat

Pal Dhaba · Sector 28

~11:00 – 23:00

The butter chicken everyone sends you to. Dhaba in name and prices, considerably busier in practice. Cash still easiest.

Sindhi Sweets · Sector 17

~08:00 – 22:00

Chole bhature, samosas and mithai in the middle of the Sector 17 plaza. The obvious breakfast when you're already shopping.

Garam Dharam · Sector 26

~12:00 – 23:00

Loud, kitsch, Punjabi-dhaba-as-theatre. Better for the group dinner than for the food, which is still perfectly good.

Backpackers Cafe · Sector 9

~11:00 – 23:30

The break from Punjabi food when nine days of it catches up with you. Continental and Asian, and comfortable for a long sit.

Delhi, briefly

Sun 1 Nov, from ~21:30 · one night only

You land at 20:15 and fly out mid-morning. This is a meal and a bed, not a stop.

Worldmark Aerocity

Many kitchens until 23:00 or later

The restaurant district a few minutes from the Aerocity hotels, and realistically your only option at that hour. Sit-down and quick-service both.

Hotel room service

24 hrs at the branded hotels

After Melbourne–Guangzhou–Delhi, the honest recommendation. Eat, sleep, and save the exploring for Bangalore.
