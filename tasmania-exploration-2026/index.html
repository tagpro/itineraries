September Travel Tips

Early spring in Tasmania rewards preparation — three things worth sorting before you go.

🌡

Weather & Layers

Spring temps ~14°C in Hobart, near freezing on mountain summits. Pack thermals and windproof jackets.

🎟

Parks Pass

Purchase a Holiday Parks Pass for Mount Field and Tasman National Parks.

🚗

Driving Advice

Daylight hours are ~12 hours (sunset ~6:00 PM). Avoid driving rural roads after dark to protect wildlife.
